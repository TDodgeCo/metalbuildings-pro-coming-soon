Plans, tools, and project support.

Here’s what I’m planning for metalbuildings.pro.

Free architectural plans

Free plans for barndominiums, shop-houses, ADUs, and other metal building projects.

Free site plan generator

Permitting a metal building is typically pretty straightforward, but the site plan is one part that commonly trips up DIY builders. The technology and tools already exist to automate much of the process. They just need to be brought together in a way that’s easy to use, even if you’ve never prepared a site plan before. I’m building that service and plan to offer it here free of charge.

PEMB consultation

I can help you plan and work through your pre-engineered metal building (PEMB) project.

  • Pricing and design, including material manufacturing, structural and foundation engineering, delivery and unloading, and installation.
  • Project rescue
  • Feasibility studies
  • Project management and coordination

Builder network

A directory of vetted industry professionals, including installation and foundation contractors, licensed architects, independent engineers, and professional project managers experienced in PEMB.
